Fabric check

Paste or photograph a care label. We estimate how much the garment may warm the groin, which is the factor with the strongest evidence, and add notes on the fibres.

This is general wellness information, not a diagnosis. Clothing is one small, reversible factor. If you have concerns about fertility, the app will guide you to the right next step.
